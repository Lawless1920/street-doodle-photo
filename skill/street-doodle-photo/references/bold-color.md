# Bold Color Mode

## Intent

Create an immediate street-poster collision using large high-chroma screen-print-like masses. Make the color fields dominate the thumbnail while the photograph remains legible.

## Color-Mass System

- Require one dominant irregular painted mass and one or two supporting masses occupying roughly 18–40% of the frame.
- Build masses from source-derived serrated bursts, slabs, puddles, torn-paper wedges, looping ribbons, chunky shadows, or oversized abstracted anatomy.
- Make at least one mass touch or cross a frame edge.
- Send one portion convincingly behind a subject through integrated depth and allow another to overlap non-critical photographic regions.
- Use two dominant hues among electric yellow, turquoise, and coral, with the third as an accent.
- Use flat opaque or semi-opaque color, rough screen-print edges, imperfect registration, scratched hatching, and visible hand wobble.
- Let lettering, line art, photographed edges, and color fields interrupt one another.
- Avoid polite circles, generic rectangles, tiny sticker spots, timid outlines, and evenly distributed decoration.

## Hierarchy

- One dominant color mass readable at thumbnail size.
- One large framing gesture, character, or phrase.
- Two to four medium inventions and several small discoveries.
- Preserve meaningful breathing room without retreating into sparse line decoration.

## Source-Aware Placement

- **Portrait:** place the dominant mass behind or beside the silhouette, let one edge cross the frame, and keep the face, eyes, hands, and identity-critical clothing readable.
- **Still life or product:** grow masses from object function, openings, reflections, liquid, packaging, or shadows while protecting real labels and logos.
- **Architecture or street:** use perspective lines, doors, wires, curbs, and signs to determine the color-mass direction rather than adding unrelated blobs.
- **Dark scene:** retain useful darkness and concentrate bold masses around one narrative cluster; do not brighten or repaint the entire photograph.
- Use one real structural edge as the composition's spine and make the supporting masses answer it across the frame.

## Quality Test

- High-chroma masses occupy about 18–40% and dominate the first read.
- A mass enters or exits a frame edge.
- Masses pass behind or overlap safe photographed regions rather than floating in empty space.
- The result feels bold, confrontational, screen-printed, and subcultural rather than polished, cute, or corporate.
- If it reads mainly as line art, revise with: `Increase opaque irregular color fields to 18–40% of the frame; make them the dominant compositional event.`
- If color hides an identity-critical subject, reduce or reroute only the offending mass; do not restart the whole composition.
