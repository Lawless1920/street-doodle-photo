# Shared Style System

## Core Idea

Treat the photograph and drawing as one authored image. Keep the photographed scene recognizable while letting an original illustrated world grow through its subjects, lighting, shadows, and geometry.

## Visual Grammar

- Preserve the source's displayed aspect ratio, orientation, full framing, scene identity, and recognizable people and objects while allowing coherent full-frame generative editing.
- Use electric yellow `#F4F13D`, bright turquoise `#35E0CF`, and coral red `#FF4B50` as the signature palette; change their proportions by mode.
- Use imperfect, porous, hand-led lines equivalent to 4–14 px at a 1000 px-wide image.
- Let marks pass plausibly behind and in front of photographed forms through integrated depth and occlusion.
- Include one exact scene-aware 1–3 word phrase by default using irregular dimensional sign lettering with turquoise outer contour, yellow inner contour, offset echo, and coral-yellow-turquoise hatched interiors.

## Shared Composition

- Build one dominant narrative gesture.
- Add two to four medium motifs anchored to separate photographed regions.
- Add five to twelve micro-marks that reward close viewing.
- Preserve one calm zone with few additions.
- Keep the eye path asymmetric and let selected strokes escape toward the frame edge.

## Motif Vocabulary

Select only motifs justified by the source:

- elongated limbs extending from people, furniture, machines, poles, bottles, cables, or shadows;
- profile heads, eyes, hands, feet, tongues, birds, serpentine creatures, smoke, and small odd companions;
- crowns, rays, sparks, impact bursts, zigzags, speed arcs, orbit lines, and puddle-like splashes;
- object labels, arrows, faux diagrams, underlines, marginal marks, imperfect checks, and scratch hatching.

Invent original shapes each time. Avoid recurring mascots unless the user adopts one intentionally.

## Scene Adaptation

- **Portrait:** frame rather than mask the face; extend clothing, hair, posture, food, or nearby objects into imaginary anatomy.
- **Street or architecture:** exploit perspective, wires, doors, signs, curbs, and windows as paths or character features.
- **Still life or product:** animate function; let handles, cords, openings, steam, reflections, labels, or shadows trigger the narrative.
- **Landscape:** use contours, clouds, water, rocks, and plant masses as quiet structural forms.
- **Crowded scene:** reduce disconnected motifs and build one coherent line or shape network.

## Shared Quality Test

- The displayed aspect ratio, orientation, complete framing, major perspective, and scene identity match the source.
- People, poses, expressions, important objects, and labels remain recognizable even though the full image may be generatively re-rendered.
- The source is immediately recognizable and at least three major additions connect to photographed geometry.
- The palette reads as yellow–turquoise with disciplined coral, not random rainbow decoration.
- Mark-making feels spontaneous and human rather than vector-perfect.
- The drawing establishes a source-specific micro-narrative.
- The phrase is correctly spelled, readable, integrated, multi-contoured, offset, irregular, and visibly hatch-filled.
- The composition contains deliberate silence and protects identity-critical details.
